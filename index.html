SwiprGallery cleaner · Android and iOS

Clean your gallery, one swipe at a time.

Swipe right to keep a photo, left to delete it. Nothing is removed straight away: deleted photos wait in a trash you can review and restore from.

  • Finds duplicates. Burst shots, edits and re-saves are grouped for you.
  • Photos and videos. Review newest first, oldest first or at random.
  • Always recoverable. Storage is only freed when you empty the trash.

ScribrTranscription and translation · Android

Speech to text that never leaves your phone.

Record or import audio and Scribr transcribes and translates it on the device. Once the speech models are downloaded it works offline, with no account.

  • Two languages, one view. Original and translation together, highlighted in time with playback.
  • Export anywhere. TXTMarkdownSRTPDFJSONCSVHTML
  • Locked if you want. Optional password encryption and a biometric app lock.

SubTrakrSubscription tracker · Android

Know what you pay for, before you pay again.

Every subscription in one list, with your monthly total, a budget to hold it to and a reminder ahead of each billing date.

  • Free trials, tracked. See the day a trial turns into a bill.
  • Any cycle, any currency. Weekly, yearly or every 45 days, shown in the currency you choose.
  • Payment history and statistics, in 20 languages.

LoadrDownload manager · Windows, macOS and Linux

Big files, done fast.

Loadr splits every file into pieces, fetches them over many connections at once and puts them back together byte for byte. Close the app, lose your Wi-Fi, restart: it picks up exactly where it stopped.
