Gallery cleaner for Android and iPhone

Clean your photo gallery, one swipe at a time.

Swipr turns clearing out thousands of photos into something quick. Swipe right to keep, left to delete. Deleted photos wait in a trash you can review and restore from, so nothing disappears by accident.

What Swipr does

Swipe to keep or delete

Go through your photos and videos one at a time. Swipe right to keep, left to delete, and hold any photo to preview it full size. You can change which direction does what.

Duplicate photo finder

Swipr groups near-identical photos automatically, including burst shots, edits and re-saves, and shows how much space you get back by removing the extras.

Blurry photo finder

A scan picks out the blurry shots in your library so you can review and remove them in one place.

Screenshots, GIFs and screen recordings

Old screenshots, GIFs, screen recordings and downloads are collected into their own lists, ready to clear out.

Clean by album or by month

Work through one album at a time, or pick a year and a month and swipe through only those photos. Swipr remembers where you stopped.

A trash you can undo

Swiped photos go to the Swipr Trash first. Restore any of them, or empty the trash when you are sure. Only then is anything removed from your phone.

Private and offline

Swipr reviews the photos stored on your phone. Your media is not uploaded, and cleaning works without an internet connection.

Made to be comfortable

Light, dark and pure black AMOLED themes, haptic feedback, and more than 20 languages.

Questions about Swipr

Does Swipr delete my photos straight away?

No. Swiping only moves a photo into the Swipr Trash. Nothing is removed from your phone until you empty the trash or delete selected items from it, and your phone may ask you to confirm.

Does Swipr upload my photos?

No. Swipr works on the photos and videos stored on your phone. Your media is not uploaded, and you can clean your gallery offline.

Will it delete my Google Photos or iCloud backup?

No. Swipr only removes the copy on your device. Photos that exist only in the cloud are skipped and left alone.

Is Swipr free?

Yes. Swipr is free to use and shows ads. A single one-time purchase removes the ads for good. There is no subscription.

Which phones does Swipr work on?

Swipr is available for Android phones on Google Play and for iPhone on the App Store.
