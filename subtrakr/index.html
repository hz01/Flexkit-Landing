Subscription tracker for Android

Know what you pay for, before you pay again.

SubTrakr keeps every subscription in one list: streaming, software, gym, insurance. You see what it all costs per month, and you get a reminder before each one renews, in time to cancel.

Get it on Google Play

What SubTrakr does

Every subscription in one list

Add a subscription in seconds from a list of popular services or type your own. Pause, resume or remove any of them, and group them by category.

Renewal reminders

Get a notification on the billing day, the day before or any number of days ahead. A weekly digest lists what renews in the next seven days.

Free trial tracking

Enter when a trial ends and SubTrakr tells you before it turns into a paid subscription.

A monthly budget

Set a spending limit and see how much of it your subscriptions use. SubTrakr warns you when you approach the limit or go over it.

Statistics and forecast

See spending by category, your most expensive subscriptions, a renewal calendar and a forecast for the next twelve months.

Any billing cycle, any currency

Weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or a custom period such as every 45 days. Totals are shown in the currency you choose.

Payment history and export

Every payment is recorded. Export your subscriptions and payment history as a CSV spreadsheet or a printable PDF report.

Shared subscriptions

Split a subscription between several people and SubTrakr counts only your share.

Optional sync and app lock

Sign in with Google to sync across devices if you want to. A biometric lock keeps the app private. Light, dark and AMOLED themes, in 20 languages.

Questions about SubTrakr

Does SubTrakr connect to my bank account?

No. You add your subscriptions yourself, so SubTrakr never needs access to your bank or your cards.

Do I need an account?

No. SubTrakr works without one. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to sync your data across devices.

Can SubTrakr cancel a subscription for me?

No. SubTrakr tracks what you pay and reminds you before each renewal, so you can cancel with the provider in time.

Is SubTrakr free?

Yes. SubTrakr is free to use and shows ads. An in-app purchase removes them.

Which devices does SubTrakr support?

SubTrakr is available for Android phones on Google Play.
